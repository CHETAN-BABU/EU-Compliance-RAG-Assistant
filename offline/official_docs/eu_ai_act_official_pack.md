# EU AI Act Official Offline Pack

Regulation: EU AI Act (EU) 2024/1689
Official source URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj

## Purpose
- Establishes harmonized EU rules for artificial intelligence.
- Promotes trustworthy, human-centric AI.
- Protects health, safety, and fundamental rights.

## Risk-Based Model
- Unacceptable-risk AI practices are prohibited.
- High-risk AI systems are subject to strict requirements.
- Limited-risk systems have transparency obligations.
- Minimal-risk systems generally have fewer obligations.

## High-Risk AI Obligations (Core)
- Risk management system.
- Data governance and quality controls.
- Technical documentation and record keeping.
- Transparency and instructions for use.
- Human oversight.
- Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity.
- Post-market monitoring and incident reporting.

## General-Purpose AI Model Obligations
- Technical documentation and downstream information duties.
- Copyright policy and compliance expectations.
- Public summary of training data content categories.
- Additional obligations for systemic-risk GPAI models.

## Governance and Enforcement
- AI Office and EU governance mechanisms support implementation.
- National competent authorities and market surveillance enforce compliance.
- Significant penalties can apply for prohibited uses and non-compliance.

## Practical Compliance Checklist
- Classify each AI use case by risk category.
- Maintain conformity and technical documentation artifacts.
- Implement human oversight controls and escalation pathways.
- Track model changes and perform impact assessments.
- Prepare transparency notices and user disclosures.

This pack is an offline guidance summary for RAG and should be validated against the official legal text for legal decisions.
