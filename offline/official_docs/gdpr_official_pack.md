# GDPR Official Offline Pack

Regulation: General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679
Official source URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

## Purpose and Scope
- GDPR sets rules on processing personal data and protects fundamental rights and freedoms.
- Applies to controllers and processors in the EU and in certain cases outside the EU when targeting EU residents.

## Core Principles (Article 5)
- Lawfulness, fairness, transparency.
- Purpose limitation.
- Data minimization.
- Accuracy.
- Storage limitation.
- Integrity and confidentiality.
- Accountability.

## Lawful Bases (Article 6)
- Consent.
- Contract necessity.
- Legal obligation.
- Vital interests.
- Public task.
- Legitimate interests.

## Data Subject Rights
- Right of access.
- Right to rectification.
- Right to erasure.
- Right to restriction.
- Right to portability.
- Right to object.
- Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling.

## Governance and Accountability
- Data protection by design and by default.
- Records of processing activities.
- Security of processing.
- Personal data breach notification obligations.
- Data protection impact assessments for high-risk processing.
- Data protection officer requirements where applicable.

## Transfers Outside EU (Chapter V)
- Adequacy decisions.
- Appropriate safeguards (including standard contractual clauses).
- Specific derogations.

## Enforcement and Penalties
- Supervisory authorities monitor and enforce GDPR.
- Administrative fines can reach up to 20,000,000 EUR or 4% of total worldwide annual turnover for severe breaches.

## Practical Compliance Checklist
- Maintain processing inventory and lawful basis mapping.
- Publish clear privacy notices.
- Implement DSAR handling process with timelines.
- Run DPIAs for high-risk use cases.
- Enforce retention and deletion policies.
- Test breach response playbook.

This pack is an offline guidance summary for RAG and should be validated against the official legal text for legal decisions.
