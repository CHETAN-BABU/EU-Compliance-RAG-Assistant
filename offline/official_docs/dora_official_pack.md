# DORA Official Offline Pack

Regulation: Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU) 2022/2554
Official source URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2554/oj

## Purpose
- Establishes a unified digital operational resilience framework for the EU financial sector.
- Focuses on ICT risk management, incident reporting, resilience testing, and third-party ICT risk.

## Core Pillars
- ICT risk management framework and governance.
- ICT-related incident classification and reporting.
- Digital operational resilience testing.
- ICT third-party risk management and contractual controls.
- Information sharing arrangements for cyber threat intelligence.

## Governance Expectations
- Management body accountability for ICT risk.
- Defined risk tolerance and resilience strategy.
- Monitoring, detection, response, and recovery capabilities.
- Backup, restoration, and business continuity planning.

## ICT Third-Party Risk
- Maintain register of ICT third-party arrangements.
- Evaluate concentration and dependency risk.
- Define exit strategies for critical or important functions.
- Ensure contracts include mandatory control and assurance clauses.

## Testing and Assurance
- Periodic resilience testing based on size and risk profile.
- Advanced threat-led testing for relevant entities.
- Lessons learned and remediation tracking.

## Practical Compliance Checklist
- Build a complete ICT asset and dependency inventory.
- Define incident severity taxonomy and reporting runbooks.
- Track third-party contractual obligations and exit readiness.
- Test recovery RTO/RPO targets and communication flows.
- Provide governance evidence for supervisory review.

This pack is an offline guidance summary for RAG and should be validated against the official legal text for legal decisions.
